Our Origin

Born from a profound love for nature

Alpha Adventures was born in February 2018, out of a profound love for nature and a desire to share its beauty with others. The seed was sown during a trek to the Chandrashila and Tunganath peak in December 2017, where the founders experienced the transformative power of nature.

We began with a small group of friends and family, and soon grew to become a trusted name in the trekking community, leading trips to the most remote and pristine locations across India. We believe that stepping out into the wild brings out the best in us, helping us discover our full potential.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

To empower individuals to step out of their comfort zones, explore the great outdoors, discover their full potential, and inspire them to live active, healthy, and meaningful lives. We make adventure travel safe, sustainable, and accessible to all.

  • Guiding individuals beyond physical and mental barriers through wilderness challenges
  • Encouraging active lifestyles and mental resilience through nature
  • Designing curated itineraries that welcome every kind of explorer
Our Horizon

Our Vision

To cultivate a community that values and protects the environment, respects local cultures, and inspires a lifelong love for outdoor exploration. We envision a future where adventure tourism and conservation walk hand in hand — where every trek leaves the trail cleaner than we found it.

  • Pioneering eco-tourism standards that leave trails cleaner than before
  • Partnering with local communities to create sustainable livelihoods
  • Becoming the benchmark for safe, low-impact outdoor travel
Our Foundation

Our Values

Principles that guide every trail, every village, and every campsite.

Safety First

Multi-layer protocols, WFR-certified leaders, and satellite tracking on every expedition. Every trek is carefully planned with trained leaders and reliable safety measures so travelers can explore with confidence.

Environmental Integrity

We follow Leave-No-Trace principles to protect fragile ecosystems for generations. From responsible trail choices to minimizing our impact, every adventure is planned with respect for nature and the places we explore.

Community & Bonding

We foster lifelong friendships through shared adventure, creating a supportive and non-competitive environment. We also respect local communities, traditions, and heritage, making every trek an opportunity to connect, learn, and create lasting memories.

Customer-First Mindset

Every itinerary, safety measure, and interaction is designed around the traveller's experience. We listen to our travelers and continuously improve our adventures to make every journey safe, comfortable, meaningful, and memorable.
