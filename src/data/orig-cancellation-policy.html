Cancellation Policy

Please note — We do not issue cash or bank refunds. If your cancellation is eligible, the amount is converted into Alpha Adventures Travel Points and reflected in your account login, as per the rules below.

This policy applies to the total cost of your trek or trip, not the advance amount you paid.

What are Alpha Adventures Travel Points?

  1. Travel Points are credits equal to your eligible refund amount, added under your name in our system.
  2. Use them as a direct discount on your next trek or trip booking.
  3. They are valid for 2 months from the date of cancellation and can be used on any future trek or trip with us.

  • No show on the day of departure
  • Cancelling after the applicable window for your trek or trip
  • Being asked to leave a trek or trip
  • Dropping out during the journey

If Alpha Adventures cancels your trek or trip

If we have to cancel a scheduled trek or trip due to circumstances beyond our control, you will first be offered a transfer to another date or to a trek/trip of equal value. If that does not work for you, we will process a 100% refund of the trek/trip fee paid to us. This refund does not cover transport costs such as train, bus or flight bookings, and any cancellation charges from the transport provider are borne by you.

This can include:

  • Extreme weather, natural disasters or environmental hazards
  • Government restrictions or closure of destinations, roads, trails or protected areas
  • Road blockages, transport strikes or accessibility issues
  • Safety, security or health related concerns in the region
  • Shortage of essential resources such as permits, accommodation or local transport

The decision to cancel, postpone, reroute, or modify an itinerary for safety and operational reasons rests solely with Alpha Adventures and will always be taken in the best interests of all participants and staff members.
